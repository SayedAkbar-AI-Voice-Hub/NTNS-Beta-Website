Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NTNS pricing.

No. Starter, Growth, and Scale plans have zero setup fees. You pay the monthly flat fee and you're live. Enterprise plans require a one-time setup investment ($2,500–$7,500) due to custom EHR/EMR integrations and dedicated infrastructure. Overage charges only apply if you exceed your plan's included minutes or SMS.

You'll be charged at your plan's overage rate for any usage beyond the included amount. Starter: $0.95/min and $0.08/SMS. Growth: $0.85/min and $0.07/SMS. Scale: $0.75/min and $0.06/SMS. Higher-tier plans have lower overage rates — so if you're regularly hitting your cap, upgrading often saves money overall.

Yes. All core plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at the end of any billing cycle with no penalties. We don't lock you in — we earn your business every month by delivering results.

Starter includes English plus one additional language (Farsi or Spanish). Growth includes all three — English, Farsi (فارسی), and Spanish — simultaneously. Scale adds smart language detection, automatically routing callers in their preferred language. Enterprise supports all three plus additional languages on request.

All plans include calendar sync (Google Calendar, Outlook) and standard CRM integration so leads and appointments flow automatically into your system. Growth and above include custom voice configuration. Scale includes multi-provider routing. Enterprise adds full custom EHR/EMR integration — we build the connection to your specific practice management software as part of the setup.

Book a 20-minute discovery call and we'll walk through your current call volume, languages, and workflows — then match you with the right plan. Most single-location clinics start on Growth. If you have multiple providers or locations, Scale is worth a look. Call the live demo first to see the AI in action for your exact industry before committing to anything.
