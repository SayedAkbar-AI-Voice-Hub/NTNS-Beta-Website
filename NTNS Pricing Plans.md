## Detailed Plan Descriptions

### Starter Plan
**Price:** $397 per month
Designed for smaller clinics handling moderate call volume who require reliable booking and reminders without excessive complexity.
*   500 inbound minutes per month
*   1 AI receptionist agent
*   Appointment booking & calendar sync
*   250 SMS reminders per month
*   English + 1 language (Farsi or Spanish)
*   Email support (48hr response time)
*   *Overage:* $0.95/min | $0.08/SMS

### Growth Plan (Recommended)
**Price:** $797 per month
Tailored for active clinics that need full automation—booking, reminders, multilingual support, and missed-call recovery all running hands-free.
*   1,000 minutes per month
*   Inbound + outbound agent
*   Book, reschedule & cancel workflows
*   750 SMS · missed-call text-back
*   English, Farsi (فارسی) & Spanish
*   Patient request routing & triage
*   Custom voice & clinic persona
*   Priority support (24hr response time)
*   *Overage:* $0.85/min | $0.07/SMS

### Scale Plan
**Price:** $1,297 per month
Ideal for multi-provider or multi-location clinics with high daily volume and complex routing needs.
*   1,500 minutes per month
*   Up to 2 AI agents (e.g., front desk + follow-up)
*   1,500 SMS · voicemail drops
*   All 3 languages + smart detection
*   Multi-provider calendar routing
*   3 custom automation workflows
*   Same-day phone & Slack support
*   *Overage:* $0.75/min | $0.06/SMS

### Enterprise Custom
**Price:** From $1,800 per month (Setup: $2.5K – $7.5K)
For clinic groups, health networks, or franchises needing custom EHR integrations and dedicated infrastructure.
*   Unlimited AI agents
*   EHR / EMR custom integration
*   Multi-language (EN / FA / ES + more)
*   White-label deployment
*   SLA-backed uptime
*   Dedicated account manager
